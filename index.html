Neutrality is a choice too, and not an innocent one. Espacio Manzana uses art as a method to walk in solidarity with the Global South and the marginalised communities in the south of Spain, giving platform to the people and causes the world too often looks away from. We don't try to convince the unconvinced. This space is for the ones who've already chosen.

ART AS THE METHOD
We don't platform art for art's sake. We work with artists, for the communities the mainstream overlooks, rooted in Andalucía, reaching the marginalised across Spain, and extending to Lebanon, Palestine, and Syria. Does your art belong here?
